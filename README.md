# A&P Auto Body and Repair Inc. website

A mobile-friendly, static one-page website built for free GitHub Pages hosting.

## Publish with GitHub Pages
1. Sign in to github.com and create a new **public** repository (for example `ap-auto-body-site`).
2. Upload the files `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` into the **root** of the repository (not inside a subfolder).
3. In the repository, go to **Settings → Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**, choose **main**, and choose **/(root)**. Save.
5. GitHub will display the website URL under **Pages**, normally `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/ap-auto-body-site/`.

## Edit your website
- Change copy, services, links, phone number, and review excerpts in `index.html`.
- Update color scheme or spacing in `style.css`.
- Replace the three `photo-placeholder` elements in the gallery with your approved shop photos, e.g. `<img src="assets/photo1.jpg" alt="A&P Auto Body completed collision repair">` and style the images.
- Replace the text-based A&P mark with your approved official logo if you have one.

## Important before launch
- The gallery intentionally contains **placeholders**, not scraped Google/Instagram images. Obtain image files you own or have rights to use; upload them to `assets/` and update HTML.
- The displayed customer review excerpts were sourced from publicly displayed Google reviews reproduced at Birdeye; verify accuracy and your right to republish before going live.
- The rating is a sourced snapshot and may change.
- Services and business hours should be confirmed by shop management before publishing any more specific promises.
- There is no backend, database, contact form, or paid feature. Telephone, directions, and Instagram links work without one.
